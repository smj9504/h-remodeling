## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** Residential homeowners in the DMV area (Maryland, Northern Virginia, Washington D.C.) seeking remodeling services. Includes English, Korean, and Chinese-speaking homeowners.

**Secondary:** Real estate agents and property managers looking for reliable remodeling partners for client referrals or property improvements.

## Product Purpose

Lead-generation and portfolio website for H Remodeling, a licensed and insured residential remodeling contractor operating in the DMV area since 2014. The site converts visitors into consultation requests by showcasing completed work, communicating expertise, and making it easy to request a free estimate. Success means a steady flow of qualified leads through the contact form and phone.

## Positioning

Two differentiators a neighboring contractor could not easily replicate:

1. **Quality + transparency:** Premium materials with honest, no-middleman pricing. The team performs the work directly rather than subcontracting, which means clearer communication and no markup layering.
2. **Bilingual service:** Full fluency in Korean and Chinese alongside English, serving immigrant homeowner communities in their own language — reflected in a fully trilingual website (EN/KO/ZH) with natural, detailed translations.

## Operating Context

- Homeowners typically discover the site through local search, referrals, or social media (Instagram, Facebook).
- The decision journey involves browsing the portfolio, reading about services, and submitting a contact form or calling directly.
- Real estate agents may browse the portfolio to evaluate quality before referring clients.
- Service areas span three jurisdictions: Maryland (Bethesda, Rockville, Silver Spring, Potomac), Northern Virginia (Arlington, McLean, Tysons, Fairfax, Falls Church, Alexandria, Vienna), and D.C. (Georgetown, Capitol Hill, Dupont Circle).
- Business hours: Mon-Fri 8AM-6PM, Sat 9AM-5PM.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Core services (4):**
1. Kitchen remodeling — custom cabinets, countertops, islands, appliances, backsplash, lighting
2. Bathroom renovation — walk-in showers, tubs, vanities, heated floors, tile, fixtures
3. Flooring installation — hardwood, LVP, tile & stone, carpet, epoxy, laminate
4. Deck building & repair — composite, wood, railings, pergolas, outdoor lighting

**Technical constraints:**
- Trilingual support (EN/KO/ZH) is a hard requirement; all user-facing content must exist in all three languages.
- Sanity CMS is integrated for portfolio management but not yet fully wired to the frontend (projects still served from static data).
- Static generation (SSG) for performance and SEO.

**Undecided:** Whether to expand services beyond the current four categories.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust through evidence:** Show real work, real numbers, and real testimonials rather than making claims. The portfolio is the primary sales tool.
2. **Language as hospitality:** Serving Korean and Chinese speakers in their own language is not a feature — it is core to the business identity and respect for the community.
3. **Friction-free contact:** Every page should make it trivially easy to call or request a quote. The conversion path must never be more than one click away.
4. **Local authority:** The site must signal deep roots in the DMV area — specific neighborhoods, local project examples, and community presence.
5. **Honest representation:** Never fabricate testimonials, inflate statistics, or claim capabilities that aren't documented.
